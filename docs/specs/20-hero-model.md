# 20 — The Hero: the user's Meshy model as the player character

**Status:** Built and verified 2026-11-07 (Editor, host and two clients through the harness); waits for the user's Multiplayer Play Mode sign-off.
**Design ref:** `docs/design/overview.md` (Character creation)

## Goal
Every player is the user's new character (Meshy AI model, Mixamo rig, 9 animations), unarmed by default and fighting with punches and kicks, painted in flat colours by body region that the character creator lets you choose.

## Decisions (user, 2026-11-07)
- Source: `Assets/_Project/Art/Meshy_AI_remove_his_armour_and_detailed_clothes_20261107_animated_mixamo_All_animation.glb` (no texture or material inside).
- **No texture: flat colours by region** (skin, hair, top, trousers, boots); the creator's colour slots colour those regions.
- **Unarmed by default: punches and kicks.** A sword comes as a pickup (spec 22). Jumping comes in spec 21.

## Scope
- In:
  - `tools/blender/hero.py` (Blender 5.1, headless): imports the GLB, splits the mesh into region materials by skin weights and height, exports `Art/Characters/Hero/Hero.fbx` with every animation as a take. Re-run after re-exporting from Meshy.
  - Unity import as **Humanoid** (Mixamo rig maps automatically); clips cut and kept in place by import settings.
  - `Hero.controller`: same parameters, states and tags as `Otherworlder.controller`, so movement, combat and networking code don't change.
    - Walk / Run ← Walking / Running.
    - Combo: Attack1, Attack2 ← two punches cut from Punch_Combo; Attack3 ← the sweep cut from Sweeping_Kick.
    - Heavy ← the kick from High_Kick; Charge ← High_Kick's chambered wind-up, held.
    - **Stand-ins** for the clips the file doesn't have: Idle, walk/run backwards and sideways, Roll, Hit, Death. They're retargeted from the Otherworlder (its FBX switched to Humanoid import) and get replaced when real ones are exported from Mixamo/Meshy.
  - Attack impact delays retuned to the new clips (data only).
  - Player prefab: the hero replaces the Otherworlder under `Model` (all players).
  - Cosmic space: the hero replaces the preview figure.
  - Appearance catalog: 5 region slots: skin, hair, top, trousers, boots. The creator shows 5 slot buttons.
- Out: a texture (none supplied); face/eye colours; shape sliders and swappable parts (spec 19 B/C need a new approach for a Meshy model); deleting the old Otherworlder/Quaternius assets (ask first).

## Requirements
- Cosmetic only: the CharacterController capsule, speeds, reach and hit timing never depend on the model.
- Hit flash, astral form, colours and lock-on work on the new model as on the old.
- The GLB stays the source; the FBX and materials are generated by the script.

## Networking

## Differences from spec
- **Facing:** the hero faces +Z (no 180° turn in the prefab).
- **Stand-in clips:** the Otherworlder's FBX import was switched to **Humanoid** so its clips retarget. They carry a 180° root rotation offset, because that model faced −Z and Humanoid keeps "Original" orientation.
- **Stand-in idle:** it's the old sword idle, so the right fist grips as if holding a hilt.
- **Walk speed:** his walk plays at 1.25× (natural pace ~1.2 m/s, where the tree places walk at 1.5 m/s); his run (~4.1 m/s) matches.
- **Import warning:** High_Kick has a little scale animation on the right thigh, which Humanoid discards. Harmless.
- **Regions:** come from the dominant bone and height, with flat cuts at the belt (0.93 m) and the boot tops (0.26 m). Sleeves reach the elbow. Hair is head-bone faces above a line that is higher in front.
- **Impact delays:** BasicAttack 0.33, ComboAttack2 0.33, ComboAttack3 0.42, HeavyAttack 0.33.
- **Old assets:** the Otherworlder and Quaternius files are still in the project, unused by the player (deleting them needs the user's OK).
